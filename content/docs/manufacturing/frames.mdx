We use wooden frames from [Nielsen](https://www.nielsen-design.de) and [Döhnert](https://www.doehnert-rahmen.de). OpenPaper 7 needs an 18 × 24 cm frame with at least 20 mm of depth.

## Nielsen

- article: `0A30N2-006-ESNL`
- profile: `A30`
- format: `18 × 24 cm`
- strip: `930520`
- spacer: `930510`
- colors: natural ash or black lime

## Döhnert

- `MO1530A11` frame, 18 × 24 cm
- `EPPKNW4015` design `1001 A`, 18 × 24 cm, 9.5 × 16 cm opening

![Paperlesspaper frames inside](./IMG_0447.jpeg)

Use good glass to keep reflections low. We recommend at least UV70 glass. Acrylic is lighter but scratches more easily.

Before assembly, take out the original backing, clean both sides of the glass, and check that the backplane fits. Do not use a warped frame or one with loose corners.

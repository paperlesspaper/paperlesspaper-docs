---
title: Parts List
description: Parts and materials needed for OpenPaper 7 and OpenPaper L.
---

## Main parts

| | OpenPaper 7 | OpenPaper L |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display | 7.3-inch Spectra 6, 800 × 480 | 13.3-inch Spectra 6, 1600 × 1200 |
| PCB | OpenPaper 7 PCB | OpenPaper L PCB |
| Batteries | 4 × AAA | 4 × AA |
| Passepartout opening | 159 × 95 mm | 268 × 200 mm |
| Display size | about 160 × 96 mm | about 270.4 × 202.8 mm |
| Frame | 18 × 24 cm, at least 20 mm deep | frame matching the display assembly |

For each device you also need:

- battery holder with cable and plug
- laser-cut 2 mm pressboard backplane
- upper and lower spacers
- USB-C support
- battery compartment and cover
- display pull tab
- double-sided tape for the PCB and battery holder
- frame clips or turn buttons

## Consumables

- black PETG-HF filament
- waterproof cyanoacrylate glue for the battery cover
- about 2 cm heat-shrink tubing per battery wire
- solder
- display cleaner and lint-free cloths
- protective paper, cushioning, and a shipping carton
- welcome flyer and setup card

The OpenPaper 7 [hardware files](https://github.com/paperlesspaper/paperlesspaper-hardware/tree/main/paper7) include the mechanical models, fixtures, laser files, Gerbers, PCB BOM, and pick-and-place file.

<Callout type="warn" title="Check the display code">
  Displays with the same size do not always use the same firmware. Check the
  exact display code before ordering or flashing a batch.
</Callout>

---
title: Passepartout
description: Cut and attach the white border around the display.
---

| | OpenPaper 7 | OpenPaper L |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | 159 × 95 mm | 268 × 200 mm |
| Material | 1 mm white card | 1 mm white card |
| Display | about 160 × 96 mm | about 270.4 × 202.8 mm |

![Paperlesspaper passepartout](./IMG_0466.jpeg)

1. Clean loose fibers from the cut edges.
2. Clean the passepartout and display edges with a lint-free cloth.
3. Remove the display film and tape liners.
4. Use the lines on the display to center the opening.
5. Lower the passepartout without sliding it across the display.
6. Press gently on the taped areas.

The white border should look even on all sides, with no tape or display edge visible.

The passepartout is the inner border of the frame that surrounds the e-ink display.

### Size and material

#### OpenPaper 7

- Cutout: 159mm \* 95mm
- Material: 1mm white cardboard
- Display: 160mm \* 96mm

#### OpenPaper L

- Cutout: 200mm \* 268mm
- Material: 1mm white cardboard

![Paperlesspaper passepartout](./IMG_0466.jpeg)

#### Assembly

1. Clean the corners of the passepartout with a microfiber cloth to remove dust and fingerprints.
2. Remove the protective film and adhesive backing from the e-ink display and PCB, being careful not to touch the display surface.
3. Then, place the passepartout over the display and PCB, ensuring that it is centered and aligned properly (there are lines on the panel).
4. Use gentle pressure to secure it in place without bending or damaging the panel.

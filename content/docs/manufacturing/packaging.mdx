---
title: Packaging
description: Instructions on how to safely package paperlesspaper hardware for shipping.
---

Package:

- Frame (wrapped in paper)
- 2 flyers
- Batteries (Germany/Austria only)
- red bubble wrap should press from the back
- "Fragile" sticker on the front and back of the package
- Shipping label on the front of the package (customs declaration if shipping outside EU)

<Callout type="warn" title="No batteries outside Germany/Austria">
  Lots of countries have strict rules about shipping LiIon batteries. They also
  sometimes make wrong assumptions for small batteries, but it is safest to
  avoid shipping them outside Germany and Austria.
</Callout>

The [hardware repository](https://github.com/paperlesspaper/paperlesspaper-hardware) contains the PCB, 3D, fixture, and laser files. These pages cover the workshop side:

1. Get the [parts](/manufacturing/bill-of-materials).
2. Make the [3D prints](/manufacturing/3d-print), [backplane](/manufacturing/laser), and [passepartout](/manufacturing/passepartout).
3. Join the [display and PCB](/manufacturing/pcb-montage).
4. Put everything into the [frame](/manufacturing/assembly).
5. [Flash and test](/manufacturing/firmware-testing) the device.
6. [Pack it](/manufacturing/packaging).

<Callout type="warn" title="Do not mix versions">
  The display, PCB, printed parts, and firmware must belong to the same model
  and hardware version.
</Callout>

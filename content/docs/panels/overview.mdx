## Portrait on OpenPaper L

Drag the divider across Eileen Collins’s cheeks and the shadow beneath her chin. Both photographs show the same spot on the panel. The tuned rendering brought skin tones closer to the source and increased facial contrast by **32%**. The two uniform skin-color samples showed more dither texture. ## Live departure board

Watch the clock in the upper right and the blue route bar below the timetable. They advance together once a second while the destinations and platforms stay in place. Separate partial updates change the expected departure time. The wide view shows the full panel and the 24-patch color reference card in real time.

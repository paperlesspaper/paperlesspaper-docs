The DEPG0730RTE730F0HP uses six native colors at 800 × 480 pixels. DKE lists it as a [7.3-inch panel](https://customer.dke.top/products/dke-73-inch-six-color-e6-epaper-display); it is used here in the OpenPaper 7.

For the photo comparisons and advice on palettes, see [Display control](/panels/dke-7-inch/display-control).

The measurements cover six solid colors and 45 dithered mixtures. These are camera-based estimates under the test lighting; see the [shared calibration guide](/panels/color-calibration) for the measurement method and how to read the swatches.

## Setup

The 7-inch panel stands to the right of the reference card on the [shared bench](/panels/overview#setup). The card overlaps part of the panel's left edge, so the test fields sit inside the visible central area.

## Field positions

Three charts place the same six solid colors at different locations. Each chart has 24 fields in the central area, within x = 104–684 and y = 80–376 in the native 800 × 480 raster. The surrounding margin keeps the sampled fields away from the card overlap and panel edges.

| Check | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Mean solid-color position difference | 2.71 ΔE₀₀ |
| Largest solid-color position difference | 14.51 ΔE₀₀ |
| Brightest / darkest sampled location in the white image | 1.37× |
| Reference-card cross-validation, mean across charts | 6.31 ΔE₀₀ |
| Largest held-out reference-card error | 16.87 ΔE₀₀ |
| Largest fraction of field pixels at a camera channel limit | 0.00% |
| Largest frame-to-frame field difference | 0.43 ΔE₀₀ |

An initial pass clipped dark channels in several dithered fields. We repeated the full series with camera saturation reduced from 128 to 96 and sharpening set to zero. The values on this page come from that second series.

The largest difference belongs to black. Its three positions differ by up to 14.51 ΔE₀₀, so treat that swatch as an average with noticeable position dependence.

The position differences include residual lighting and camera effects. This test does not separate field position from refresh history. The white-image correction compensates for a brightness gradient, but cannot remove reflected glare. No measurements from the 13.3-inch panel are used to calculate these values.

## Measurement notes

The reference-card cross-validation is about 6.31 ΔE₀₀ on average, with a largest held-out error of 16.87 ΔE₀₀. Black varies substantially across positions, as shown above. Each mixture was measured at one position, so the solid-color checks do not establish repeatability for every mixture.

See the [measurement limits](/panels/color-calibration#measurement-limits) before using these values for color matching or comparing panels.

## Photo rendering tests

On 21 November 2026, OpenPaper 7 (`frame-1`, stock firmware 3.0.45) showed two versions of the same portrait and coffee photograph in one refresh, each at 166 × 166 native pixels. The next refresh swapped their positions. Eight uniform target patches shared the same raster. The bench lamp was off for all four captures. [Side-by-side photo comparisons](/panels/dke-7-inch/display-control#measured-colors-in-the-ditherer).

| Paired test | Renderer | Grain A | Grain B | Mean grain | Mean photo error (ΔE₀₀) |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Palette | Ideal RGB, Floyd–Steinberg | 7.64 | 7.73 | 7.69 | 5.42 |
| Palette | Measured colors, Floyd–Steinberg | 7.23 | 7.58 | 7.40 | 3.33 |
| Dithering | Measured colors, Floyd–Steinberg | 7.17 | 7.82 | 7.49 | 3.58 |
| Dithering | Measured colors, nearest color per pixel | 0.85 | 0.80 | 0.83 | 10.41 |

<PanelPhoto src="/panels/dke-7-inch/2026-11-21/flat-patch-comparison.png" width={848} height={385} alt="Magnified camera crops of darker skin, dark brown, brick red and teal with ideal and measured six-color palettes" caption="Each column pair compares the two palettes. The two rows come from position-swapped refreshes. Dark brown and teal are smoother with the measured palette. Brick red shows more texture." />

Both dithered renderers used serpentine Floyd–Steinberg in linear RGB. Grain is the RMS of the fine Lab variation on flat fields after subtracting a Gaussian blur (σ = 2 panel pixels). Photo error compares 12 × 12 pixel block averages with the source scaled to the local paper white and black. Lower values are better for both measures. Each capture used a Spyder Checkr 24 camera profile. Mean held-out chart error ranged from 5.6 to 6.0 ΔE₀₀, which limits absolute color accuracy. The portrait of Eileen Collins (NASA) and the coffee image (Rachel Michetti) come from [scikit-image](https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.data.html).

In the second series the firmware reported full draws of 42.068 and 42.051 seconds. It disabled quick refresh at reported main-board temperatures of 19.7 °C and 20.7 °C. The serial start command and USB auto-reset did not establish a ROM loader connection for the separate raw-code trial. All photo results here use the six standard codes.

The JSON files list the two photographs in portrait, coffee order. Patch order is light skin, darker skin, dark brown, brick red, orange, gray, teal and blue-gray. Within each capture, mode order follows the corresponding two table rows. [Palette readings (JSON)](/panels/dke-7-inch/2026-11-21/six-profile-profiled.json) · [Dithering readings (JSON)](/panels/dke-7-inch/2026-11-21/dithering-profiled.json).

## Default photo settings vs. moderate boost

This comparison uses the **photo editor’s default Auto / Natural settings** in epdoptimize 1.4.1. We rendered the 312 × 208 RGB crop with the editor’s default option builder and `aitjcizeSpectra6Palette`, then converted the result to the native RGB codes. For this image the default selected serpentine Floyd–Steinberg with RGB matching. The tuned version used the same source pixels, the measured six-color palette and the moderate curve below. Firmware 3.0.45 performed an ordinary full refresh in both cases. [Compare the two panel photographs](/panels/dke-7-inch/display-control#default-photo-settings-vs-tuned-rendering).

| Rendering | Chroma C* | Lightness contrast ΔL* | Mean color error ΔE₀₀ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Editor default · Auto / Natural | 48.73 | 9.23 | 20.73 |
| Measured palette + moderate boost | 34.20 | 12.20 | 5.37 |

Both crops appeared together in two refreshes on frame-1, swapping positions on the second. The bench lamp was off. The slider uses a perspective-corrected camera crop from the same physical position in each refresh, with no color or contrast adjustment. We averaged two settled camera readings per refresh over 498 orange-dominated blocks, using the definitions below. Mean held-out reference-card error ranged from 5.5 to 5.8 ΔE₀₀. The separate trials below also measured grain on uniform patches. ## Orange photo contrast and chroma

All three versions of Evan-Amos’s orange photograph shared each full refresh on frame-1. Across three refreshes each version occupied every position once. The bench lamp was off. Each photograph used 312 × 208 native pixels. [Compare the moderate boost](#moderate-boost-against-the-calibrated-reference) · [Compare the strong boost](#strong-boost-against-the-calibrated-reference) · [Original orange photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Orange-Fruit-Pieces.jpg).

| Rendering | Chroma C* | Lightness contrast ΔL* | Photo error ΔE₀₀ | Patch grain |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Calibrated reference | 28.56 | 10.63 | 5.30 | 10.04 |
| Moderate boost | 34.50 | 13.25 | 5.12 | 9.48 |
| Strong boost | 38.82 | 14.91 | 6.72 | 9.52 |

Chroma is the mean C* of 498 orange-dominated 8 × 8 pixel blocks. Contrast is the span from the 10th to the 90th percentile of L* in those blocks. Color error compares them with the original scaled to the local paper white and black. Grain measures fine Lab variation on eight uniform orange patches. We averaged two settled camera readings per refresh and then averaged the three positions. [Per-capture measurements (JSON)](/panels/dke-7-inch/2026-11-21/oranges/contrast-measurements.json).

The renderer applied an S-curve to lightness in the measured paper range and scaled Lab chroma around the paper’s gray axis. For normalized lightness `t`, the curve was `t^g / (t^g + (1 − t)^g)`. The moderate setting used `g = 1.25` and a chroma multiplier of 1.30. The strong setting used 1.50 and 1.65.

## Red/yellow mixtures in the orange photo

A separate trial tuned the mixture to the image’s orange areas. Other pixels kept the reference rendering. Each pair shared a refresh and swapped positions in a repeat. With neutral pixels available, mean orange photo error fell from 4.69 to 2.07 ΔE₀₀ and mean grain across eight patches rose 54%. With only red and yellow in the orange areas, patch grain fell 14%, while photo error rose from 4.51 to 5.04 ΔE₀₀.

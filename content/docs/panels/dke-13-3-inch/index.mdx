The DEPG1330RTE133F5HP is a 13.3-inch Spectra 6 panel with a 1600 × 1200 pixel test raster. We measured its six solid colors and 45 dithered mixtures using a camera and a Spyder Checkr 24 reference card.

These are camera-based estimates under the test lighting. See the [Panels overview](/panels/overview) for the shared setup, method, and how to read the swatches.

Jump to the [display control measurements](#display-control-measurements) for the refresh and grayscale trials. For settings and a working photo LUT example, see [Display control](/panels/dke-13-3-inch/display-control).

## Setup

The 13.3-inch panel sits to the left of the reference card on the [shared bench](/panels/overview#setup). The measurement charts were moved toward the center to avoid reflections at the corners.

## Solid colors

Each color was measured at three positions near the center of the panel. The swatches show the average. The input code is the RGB value sent to the panel: `#FF0000`, for example, produced the red measured here as `#A3212C`.

## Reflections and field positions

The first charts covered most of the panel and caught reflections in the upper-left and lower-right corners. We repeated the measurements with smaller fields near the center: three charts of 24 fields, within x = 360–1252 and y = 250–958 in panel pixels.

Each chart places the six solid colors in different positions. Together, the charts cover all 45 two-color mixtures, plus white, black, and three-color controls.

| Check | Original layout | Central layout |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mean solid-color position difference | 4.32 ΔE₀₀, two positions | 1.62 ΔE₀₀, three position pairs per color |
| Largest solid-color position difference | — | 4.28 ΔE₀₀ |
| Brightest / darkest sampled location in the white image | 1.96× | 1.34× |
| Reference-card cross-validation, mean | About 4.7 ΔE₀₀ | About 4.5 ΔE₀₀ |

Moving the fields improved agreement between positions. Because the layouts use different positions and sample counts, this is a repeatability check rather than a measure of absolute accuracy.

Sixteen observations from the original charts overlapped the reflection regions and were excluded from comparisons with the new fields. The original solid-color position statistic remains in the table as a baseline. The excluded rectangles are `[0, 0, 400, 220]` and `[1260, 900, 1600, 1200]`, in `[left, top, right, bottom]` panel coordinates.

## Display control measurements

These trials used the experimental firmware on `frame-2`. BUSY times cover the panel drive. They exclude image transfer and power-up. The full-frame recordings were aligned at the start of the drive and captured at roughly 7–8 frames per second. Short flashes can fall between captured frames.

### Full and partial refresh timings

| Trial | BUSY |
| --- | ---: |
| Full color, normal dynamic clock | 35.825 s |
| Full color, nominal 75 Hz | 23.857 s |
| Full color, nominal 120 Hz | 14.930 s |
| Full monochrome, fast clock | 14.927 s |
| Initial 240 × 400 partial window, fast clock | 14.944 s |
| Direct comparison of refresh arguments `0x00` and `0x01` | 14.948 s each |

Other normal refreshes took about 27 s on this panel. In the initial window trial the exterior faded even when its image RAM contents stayed the same. The “VCOM” label in the comparison identifies the argument byte sent with display refresh `0x12`. With `0x01`, the sampled exterior patches stayed close to their initial readings across repeated changes.

### Digits and clock markers

Across five black/white steps, black reached 90% of its measured optical change at 9.90–9.92 s and white at 13.50–13.53 s. BUSY remained active. The cutoff was a hardware reset timed from the falling BUSY edge. For **8 → 1 → 2 → 7**, black ran for 10 s and each white erase finished. The mean difference between old strokes and the blank comparison area ranged from −0.18 to +1.69 camera-luma counts (0–255) across four erases. Individual segments showed larger differences.

| Trial | Tick interval | Camera observations after 60 ticks |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| One 100 ms pulse, `MAPDIAL 1 22116655` | 0.997–1.000 s | 60 tips detectable, 3.7% of face classified as changed, residual yellow ring |
| Two 100 ms pulses, `MAPDIAL 2 2751A351` | 1.247–1.250 s | 59 tips detectable, 33.9% of face classified as changed |
| Blue marker | About 1 s | 59 tips had strong dark contrast, 19 met the blue criterion, 18 met both |

### Letter-by-letter animation

The same 1488 × 444 logo fits across 93% of the panel width. Each of its 14 letters appears in a separate update. Drawing time runs from the start of the first step to the controller's completion report. We checked the finished logo against the camera recording. Each row is a single run on `frame-2` and excludes the white preparation.

| Playback | Outline | Partial updates | Pulse | Drawing time |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Streamed from the host | 12 segments | 26 | 100 ms | 20.37 s |
| Preloaded on the device | 12 segments | 26 | 100 ms | 14.90 s |
| Preloaded on the device | 12 segments | 26 | 60 ms | 13.87 s |
| Reuse the reset at pulse cutoff | 12 segments | 26 | 100 ms | 13.21 s |
| Reuse the reset at pulse cutoff | 12 segments | 26 | 60 ms | 12.11 s |
| Reuse the reset at pulse cutoff | 12 segments | 26 | 40 ms | 11.46 s |
| Draw one whole edge per update | 4 edges | 18 | 60 ms | 8.50 s |
| Draw the outline with the lower letters | No separate outline steps | 14 | 60 ms | **6.38 s** |

With 12 outline segments, shortening the pulse from 60 to 40 ms saved 0.65 s and made the blue letters lighter. Median camera luma inside the letters rose from 65 to 70 on a 0–255 scale. The white reference measured 169 in both runs. [Three runs side by side (video)](/panels/dke-13-3-inch/control/letter-by-letter-pulse-comparison.mp4) · [Four-edge animation (video)](/panels/dke-13-3-inch/control/letter-by-letter-four-edges.mp4).

The fastest complete run used `PLOTREADY 60` in bench firmware v39. It waited for the power-on BUSY cycle before every pulse and omitted the extra 30 ms settling delay after the first letter. The median interval between letter starts was 0.441 s. White preparation took 38.50 s. The camera captured 95 frames during drawing at 1440 × 2560 sensor pixels. The landscape video rotates them to 2560 × 1440 and preserves their timestamps. [Watch the complete run](/panels/dke-13-3-inch/display-control#letter-by-letter-animation).

**Failed power-on trial.** With `PLOTDIRECT 60`, the firmware skipped power-on after the first letter. It logged all 14 cutoff events in 2.53 s, but the camera showed only the first P.

### Red profile trials

The red-window trials varied window size, the programmed temperature hint and `CCSET` (`0xE0`). Drive periods clustered at 14.9–15.1 s and 23.5–23.8 s. Returning to the initial setting restored approximately 15 s. The observed phases do not identify a particular factory waveform bank. Panel temperature was not measured or swept in a chamber.

### Color and grayscale comparisons

All 16 image codes were tested after both white and black preparation. Some short pulses approached orange from a particular starting state. No single code produced repeatable solid orange on completion. The 20 tiled skin-tone mixtures fell into five measured brightness groups.

<PanelPhoto src="/panels/dke-13-3-inch/control/orange.webp" width={880} height={508} alt="Uniform pixel codes compared with red, yellow and spatially mixed orange reference patches" caption="Uniform pixel codes beside red, yellow and spatially mixed orange reference fields." />

The repeated three-level photo and text test gave a white-patch camera luma of 188.9–189.5 against 190.2 in the ordinary monochrome render. Some direct midtone recipes had a sepia cast. Mixing recipes and adjusting the source tone curve reduced it.

<PanelPhoto src="/panels/dke-13-3-inch/control/text-antialias.webp" width={344} height={352} alt="Enlarged source pixels showing gray samples along the edge of a letter" caption="Source pixels along the letter edge show where the renderer assigns gray." />

The Apollo photo occupied 1040 × 1040 individually addressed pixels. The bright eight-level version used serpentine Floyd–Steinberg to select the tone and ordered tiles for mixed levels. Both measured ramps in the 16-mixture trial increased monotonically. On matching smooth patches of the suit, coarse brightness variation fell about 7% and fine variation rose about 13% relative to eight levels. Of the six direct recipes, the two darkest nearly coincided. The reference strip below the photo belongs to the test pattern.

### Uniform gray recipes

Each field received a preparation code during a full drive, then a target code during a timed partial update. Each of the 256 pairs was sampled at one position per pulse time (2.1, 2.8, 3.6 and 4.6 s). Selected pairs were repeated on larger fields at additional times from 1.85 to 2.45 s.

| Recipe | Pulse | Position-corrected camera luma between the old gray and white |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `0xFD` | 2.8 s | 23–24%, cool gray. Repeated at 15 positions. Median still 24% after 79 s. |
| `0x8D` | 2.8 s | 36–40%, brighter and bluer, across five fields. |
| `0x09` | 1.85 s | 84–88%, pale and slightly warm, in one five-position run. |
| `0x09` | 2.1 s | 54–57% at three right-hand positions and about 90% at two left-hand positions. Moving the patches reproduced the difference. |

The scale runs from gray (`0x8A`) to white (`0x97`), both at 2.8 s, and describes relative camera brightness. Absolute reflectance was not measured. Two bright reference-card patches clipped in the 1.95 s capture. [All 1,024 measurements with times, pixel codes, field positions and camera readings (CSV)](/panels/dke-13-3-inch/control/pixel-recipes.csv).

To repeat the `FD` trial, send the line below followed by a newline to the frame-2 bench console at 115200 baud. The first argument is the partial drive time in milliseconds, starting at the active BUSY edge after `0x12`. The five bytes specify alternating samples of `FD`, old gray and white. Full preparation cycles precede the timed drive.

```text
PATCHGRID 2800 FD8AFD97FD
```

The photo encoder can also decode an existing command back to CSV. Pass the quoted command line to `python3 patch_palette.py decode`. [Photo LUT example and encoder](/panels/dke-13-3-inch/display-control#setting-a-custom-lut).

### Controller waveform references

Seeed's NT61522-family driver describes chip-on-glass OTP waveform storage. Its 13.3-inch driver supplies initialization, image codes and a refresh command. Neither the driver nor these optical trials establish a verified host command for loading the internal phase table.

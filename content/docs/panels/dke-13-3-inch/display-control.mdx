These results come from one 13.3-inch OpenPaper L (`frame-2`, DKE DEPG1330RTE133F5HP) running experimental bench firmware. [Test data and recordings](/panels/dke-13-3-inch#display-control-measurements).

## Full-frame speed, flashing and color

All three settings flash during a full-color refresh. Register `0x30` selects the clock. BUSY time measures the panel drive after image transfer.

| Clock setting | Register value | BUSY in this comparison | Settled color |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Normal (dynamic) | `0x08` | 35.8 s | Reference |
| Nominal 75 Hz | `0x04` | 23.9 s | Green shifts toward yellow |
| Nominal 120 Hz | `0x07` | 14.9 s | Blue becomes paler and shows a vertical band |

## Partial updates and the area outside the window

A window covering **5% of the panel still took about 15 seconds**. Sending `0x01` with the refresh command (`0x12`) kept the surrounding dark, red and blue patches close to their original appearance during repeated black/white updates. The active window still flashed.

## Early cutoff, digits and a one-second hand

For changing digits, the firmware interrupted black after **10 s** with a hardware reset and let white finish to BUSY-ready. Cutting white at 13.5 s left the first erase visibly darker. [Digit video and ghosting measurements](/panels/dke-13-3-inch#digits-and-clock-markers).

A short marker at the edge of the clock advanced **once per second for 60 ticks** on a 100 ms drive pulse per tick. The dial avoided a full-screen flash. A yellow ring remained after one revolution. The two-pulse version took 1.25 s per tick and left more of the face marked. [Blue-marker video](/panels/dke-13-3-inch/control/blue-marker.mp4).

## Red transitions, factory profiles and temperature

Changing `CCSET` (`0xE0`) and the temperature hint produced no fast, red-only update that completed without reset. Ripple and Sparkle command sequences remain unverified. [Profile timings and red-window video](/panels/dke-13-3-inch#red-profile-trials).

## Native colors, orange and skin tones

The panel produces black, white, red, yellow, blue and green. Orange required mixing adjacent red and yellow pixels for a repeatable result. For pale pink, a 4 × 4 tile used 14 white pixels and two red ones.

## Grayscale photographs, text and dithering

Gray edge pixels smoothed text while the white background stayed close to the monochrome reference. In the Apollo photograph, eight mixed levels preserved the folds in the bright sleeve. Sixteen reduced coarse grain but added fine texture. Six directly driven tones without dithering lost detail in the highlights.

## Finding a lighter gray without dithering

**`0xFD` at 2.8 s** gave a cool gray about a quarter of the way from the previous gray to white. The three center fields below use this same recipe. `0x09` produced a paler tone, but at 2.1 s its brightness varied across the panel. ## Setting a custom LUT

**You can edit the photo LUT in the bench firmware.** It maps image brightness to two-pass pixel recipes. For example, `FD` prepares a pixel with code `F` and drives it with `D` during the timed update. Loading custom voltage waveforms into the NT61522 remains unverified.

1. Save the [example photo LUT (CSV)](/panels/dke-13-3-inch/control/photo-lut.csv?build=6382664) and [Python encoder/decoder](/panels/dke-13-3-inch/control/patch_palette.py) in the same directory.
2. Edit the two-hex-digit `recipe` and its inclusive brightness limit, `upper_luma` (0–255). Use 16 rows with nondecreasing thresholds ending at 255. Repeat 255 to fill unused rows.
3. Generate the command in that directory.

```sh
python3 patch_palette.py photo photo-lut.csv
```

Send the printed `FINEPHOTO` line followed by a newline to the bench serial console at 115200 baud. It renders the built-in Apollo photo with your table and a 2.8 s partial pulse. [Photo rendered with the example LUT](/panels/dke-13-3-inch/control/apollo-host-lut.webp?build=6382664).
